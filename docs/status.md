## The one thing to know

Everything below is headless-, software-render- and (where stated) real-backend-verified. **Headset testing is ongoing and informal, and this repo does not record which features have been checked by hand**; nothing has been measured on Quest hardware (PCVR via Virtual Desktop is the main route so far). "Built" does not mean "good in the hand". When the owner reports a feature checked or a bug found, record it here and in the [status log](status-history/status-log.md) (its "Untested paths" section is the older open-verification list).

8. **Pattern copies in VR (3 Nov, V1-V3; see the Mates row):** known limits. The copies of a patterned part follow their source frame by frame during a live drag (S3, 3 Nov) once the scope's component patterns are read (one request at the first drag; until then, and for a copy that is not a top-level source's, they stay put) and the drag's commit puts them where the server says (before S3 a plain drag's commit did not settle them at all; a solve, a mate edit, Enforce all and a sandbox return still do). A solve inside a sub-assembly refreshes the copies under every loaded root that has that Part below it (S4). The live pose is computed here from the pattern rows (`pattern_copy_pose.gd`), checked against the backend's numbers to 1e-9, but it is the dragged part's projected pose, so a hitch in that pose is a hitch in the copies; a drag of a part INSIDE a sub-assembly whose copies stand under the world root (`group_root` is not the Part that owns the dragged node) does not move those copies live, they catch up at the commit. A group solve of the part mated to a copy may move the copy's source too (the backend's followers; Minimal movement, on by default, tries moving only the part first). VR does not notice a pattern edit or a Dissolve made in the flat app until the assembly is reloaded (as for any other Flutter edit). `status` is computed by the backend for every mate: on DIDSA-CAD's own frame fixture four of its sixteen mates are reported broken because a face they reference does not resolve, so a loaded file can show broken rows that were already broken. Headless- and live-backend-tested; not checked on the headset.
